## Figure set argument

The integrated figure set supports one bounded claim: monitor-gated value
filtering improves and diagnoses GeoJEPA-MPC in real farmland planning
environments, while robust cross-region transfer superiority is not robustly
supported by the current Dongxing evidence.

Claim boundary: Bishan-initialized transfer superiority is not robustly supported
and must not be written as the integrated Paper10 result.

## Figure 4. Dongxing return-label scaling

**Core conclusion.** In Dongxing/Neijiang, real-environment return labels raise
mean reward for both transfer and scratch families from pairwise-only labels to
50x16 return labels.

**Evidence chain.**

| panel | content | job |
|---|---|---|
| 4a | grouped reward bars by label type and family | Show reward improvement from pairwise-only to 20x16 and 50x16 labels. |
| 4b | optional supplementary table from Table 4 | Preserve slope, contiguity, and baimu-area tradeoffs. |

**Archetype.** Quantitative grid.

**Source data.**

- `e0_dongxing_return_label_family_summary_2026-06-10.csv`
- `e0_dongxing_return_label_50x16_family_2026-06-10.md`
- `scripts/paper10/plot_integrated_dongxing_figures.py`

**Caption draft.** Dongxing/Neijiang reward response to return-label scaling.
Bars show mean 100-step reward across three initialization checkpoints and five
rollout seeds per checkpoint; error bars show the reported reward standard
deviation across the 15 episodes. Return labels improve both transfer and
scratch families, but the 50x16 scratch family remains higher than transfer,
so this panel supports calibration rather than transfer superiority.

**Review risk.** Do not claim that Bishan initialization robustly outperforms
Dongxing scratch adaptation; the current Figure 4 source data show the opposite
at 50x16.

## Figure 5. Dongxing low-label transfer stress test

**Core conclusion.** Dongxing low-label transfer is mixed: scratch is higher at
5 and 10 labelled states, while transfer is higher at 20 labelled states.

**Evidence chain.**

| panel | content | job |
|---|---|---|
| 5a | reward versus label budget for transfer and scratch | Show the non-monotone transfer boundary. |
| 5b | optional supplementary table from Table 5 | Preserve slope, contiguity, and baimu-area tradeoffs. |

**Archetype.** Quantitative grid.

**Source data.**

- `e0_dongxing_low_label_budget_family_summary_2026-06-10.csv`
- `e0_dongxing_low_label_budget_family_2026-06-10.md`
- `scripts/paper10/plot_integrated_dongxing_figures.py`

**Caption draft.** Dongxing low-label-budget transfer stress test. Points show
mean reward for transfer and scratch families when the 50x16 return-label file
is truncated to 5, 10, or 20 labelled states; error bars show the reported
reward standard deviation across the 15 rollout episodes. Scratch is higher at
5 and 10 labels, whereas transfer is higher at 20 labels. This supports a
bounded stress-test interpretation, not a robust transfer-superiority claim.

**Review risk.** The figure should keep the 5-label and 10-label scratch
advantage visible; collapsing the panel to the 20-label result would overstate
the transfer claim.
